# Up and Out — Rooms & Image Prompts

38 canonical rooms in the same order and wall grouping as your nano-banana button JSON, plus the 7 optional legend floors at the end. Every prompt is self-contained (paste as-is). Ids match `up-and-out-room-requests.json`.

Shared visual grammar baked into every prompt: scratchy dip-pen ink, loose splashy watercolour, lots of white paper, wobbly lines, tiny wiry Oompa-Loompas, no text in the image, no photorealism.

---

## Wall 1

**1. The Chocolate Room** — `chocolate-room`
Canon: chocolate river mixed by waterfall, sugar-grass meadow, pink boiled-sweet boat.
Prompt: A vast underground meadow seen through the open doors of a glass lift: a brown river with a churning chocolate waterfall, candy-striped grass, spindly lollipop trees, a tiny pink boat, a few wiry little workers waving. Scratchy dip-pen ink, loose splashy watercolour, lots of white paper, wobbly joyful lines, no text, in the illustrated style of Quentin Blake.
